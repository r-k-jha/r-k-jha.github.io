01 About

WHERE COMPUTATION MEETS CURIOSITY

Understanding nature, From first principles to new discoveries.

Currently pursuing final year dissertation at the "Indian Institute of Technology, Mandi" as part of my M.Sc. in Physics (Computational Physics) 2025-2027, at the "Central University of Punjab".

My interests lie at the intersection of condensed matter physics, computational materials science and quantum phenomena. I use first-principles computational methods to investigate how the microscopic structure of matter determines its electronic and magnetic behaviour.

My current research direction involves density functional theory, magnetic tunnel junctions and quantum spintronics.
